About Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa

A relaxing beachfront resort in Langma, Kokrobite — pool, spa, restaurant and bar, right by the ocean.

Our Resort

Relaxation, beachfront experiences and warm hospitality

Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa is a 3-star beachfront resort in Langma, Kokrobite, on the coast of Greater Accra. The resort has a private beach area, an outdoor pool, a spa, a restaurant and a bar — everything a guest needs for a relaxing stay by the ocean.

Rooms are air-conditioned with private bathrooms, free Wi-Fi and laptop-friendly workspaces. The front desk is open 24 hours, and the team is there whenever you need them.

Our hospitality philosophy

We believe a good stay is a simple one: a clean, comfortable room, food and drink on site, a pool and a spa to unwind in, and staff who are there when you need them. With the beach right outside and the ocean in view from the pool, the resort is built for slow days and good rest.

What makes Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa a destination worth visiting

The resort sits on the coast at Langma, with a private beach area, palm trees and the Atlantic Ocean right there. Guests can swim in the outdoor pool, book a massage at the spa, eat at the restaurant, have a drink at the beach bar, or simply sit on the patio and watch the sea.

Relaxation and wellness

The spa offers massage, deep-tissue massage, hot stone massage, sports massage and Swedish massage. Treatment durations and prices are confirmed by the resort — request an appointment and the resort will confirm your session.

Dining and leisure

The resort has a restaurant, a bar and a beach bar. Breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 9:30 AM — it is included with some room types, or available for an extra charge. The restaurant serves a dinner buffet, and there is late-night room service. There is also a conference center and an outdoor entertainment area for events and gatherings.

Our location

The resort is in Langma, Kokrobite, in the Greater Accra Region — on the coast near Kokrobite Beach, with Bojo Beach also nearby. The resort is child-friendly, with a kids’ pool, babysitting and activities for kids.

What to expect as our guest

A warm welcome at reception, a comfortable air-conditioned room, and a pool, a spa and a restaurant to enjoy. If you need anything during your stay — an early check-in, a late check-out, a taxi, or a table for dinner — ask at reception.

Good to know: the resort only accepts cash. The minimum check-in age is 18. Pets are not allowed. The resort does not have an elevator. Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is before 12:00.
