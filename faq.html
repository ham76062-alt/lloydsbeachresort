FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions guests ask most about Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa, Kokrobite.

How can I reserve a room?

Use the booking form on this website — your enquiry opens in WhatsApp to send to the resort on 055 797 4777 — or call the resort on 055 797 4777. Your enquiry is also shown back to you on the page. The resort will confirm availability and your reservation. The form alone does not confirm a booking — the resort confirms it.

What room categories are available?

Seven room types are shown on this website: the Standard Double Room (65 m², 1 double bed, sleeps 2), the Deluxe Suite (85 m², 1 double bed, sleeps 2), the Queen Suite (125 m², 1 double bed, sleeps 2), the King Room (200 m², 1 extra-large double bed, sleeps 2), the One-Bedroom Apartment (185 m², 1 large double bed, sleeps 2), the Family Suite (220 m², 1 double bed and 1 extra-large double bed) and the Family Two-Bedroom Apartment with View (230 m², 1 double bed and 1 extra-large double bed). All rooms are non-smoking, with air conditioning and a private bathroom, and breakfast is included.

What are the room rates?

Room rates start from GH₵818 per night, including taxes and charges: Standard Double Room GH₵818, Deluxe Suite GH₵960, Queen Suite GH₵1,114, King Room GH₵1,541, One-Bedroom Apartment GH₵2,429, Family Suite GH₵2,062, Family Two-Bedroom Apartment with View GH₵3,022. Breakfast is included with these rates. The resort also offers an all-inclusive getaway for two — GH₵2,799 for 3 days and 2 nights, with free Wi-Fi, bed and breakfast, a massage session, lunch and dinner, pool access and welcome drinks.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is before 12:00. The front desk is open 24 hours.

What are the pool hours?

Pool access is from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Whether day visitors may use the pool is confirmed by the resort.

What spa treatments are available?

The spa offers massage, deep-tissue massage, hot stone massage, sports massage and Swedish massage. Treatment durations and prices are confirmed by the resort — request an appointment on the Spa page.

What dining is available at the resort?

The resort has a restaurant, a bar and a beach bar. Breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 9:30 AM — it is included with some room types, or available for an extra charge. The restaurant serves a dinner buffet, and there is late-night room service. The menu, food prices and opening hours are confirmed by the resort.

Is parking available?

Yes — free parking is available on site for guests.

How can I contact the resort?

Call the resort on 055 797 4777. The resort is in Langma, Kokrobite, Greater Accra Region, Ghana (plus code FJJ5+G2 Kokrobite). No email address has been published for the resort yet.

Still have a question? Contact us or call 055 797 4777.
