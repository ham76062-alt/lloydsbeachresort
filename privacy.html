Privacy Policy

How Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa handles the information you share with us.

Information you give us

When you send a booking enquiry, a spa request, an event enquiry or a message through this website, you share your name, phone number and any details you write in the form. A booking enquiry opens in WhatsApp with your details, so you can send it to the resort on 055 797 4777 yourself. A spa request, an event enquiry or a message is not sent anywhere — it is shown back to you on the page, so you can call the resort on 055 797 4777 and read out the details yourself.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, confirm a reservation or an appointment, and contact you about your stay. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else for marketing.

What we do not collect

This website does not create accounts, does not take payments, and does not store your details in a database.

Contact

If you have any questions about your information, call the resort on 055 797 4777.
