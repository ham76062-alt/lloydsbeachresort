Beachfront Experience

Where the Ocean Meets Relaxation

The resort sits on the coast at Langma, with a private beach area, palm trees and the Atlantic Ocean right there.

The Coast

Beachfront, all day long

Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa sits on the coast at Langma, Kokrobite, with a private beach area. From the pool deck and the beachside patio you can see the ocean — and the beach is right there for a walk, a swim or a quiet sit in the shade.

The resort has relaxing outdoor spaces, beach walks and sunset experiences. Kokrobite Beach and Bojo Beach are nearby, and the resort runs a beach shuttle for guests.

  • Private beach area
  • Beachside patio with a covered seating area
  • Beach walks and sunset experiences
  • Beach shuttle for guests
  • Outdoor entertainment area
