Restaurant & Bar

A restaurant, a bar and a beach bar at Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa — food and drinks by the beach.

Dining

Eat and drink by the beach

The resort has a restaurant, a bar and a beach bar. Breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 9:30 AM — it is included with some room types, or available for an extra charge. The restaurant serves a dinner buffet, and there is late-night room service.

The menu, food prices and opening hours are confirmed by the resort — enquire and the resort will tell you what is on.
