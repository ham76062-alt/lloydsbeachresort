Welcome to Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa

A relaxing coastal destination in Kokrobite

Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa is a 3-star beachfront resort in Langma, Kokrobite, on the coast of Greater Accra. The resort sits right by the beach, with an outdoor pool, a spa, a restaurant and a bar — a place to slow down, swim, eat well and enjoy the ocean.

Rooms are air-conditioned with private bathrooms, free Wi-Fi and laptop-friendly workspaces. The front desk is open 24 hours, and the team is there whenever you need them.

  • Beachfront resort with a private beach area
  • Outdoor pool with a kids’ pool, open 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM
  • Spa with massage, deep-tissue, hot stone, sports and Swedish treatments
  • Restaurant, bar and beach bar on site
  • Free Wi-Fi, free parking and a 24-hour front desk

Beachfront Experience

Where the Ocean Meets Relaxation

The resort sits on the coast at Langma, with a private beach area, palm trees and the Atlantic Ocean right there. Walk on the beach, sit under the covered patio, or watch the sunset from the pool deck.

Guests can enjoy beach walks, relaxing outdoor spaces and the ocean view from the pool and the patio. The beach is the heart of the resort — and it is right outside.

Experience the Coast — Book Your Stay

Swimming Pool

Cool off by the pool, steps from the ocean

The resort has an outdoor pool with turquoise water, white lounge chairs and green umbrellas, shaded by palm trees — with the ocean just behind it. There is also a kids’ pool, and an adult-only pool area with a lifeguard.

Pool access is from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. The pool deck is the place to spend a slow afternoon: swim, sunbathe, and look out at the sea.

Relax. Recharge. Reconnect.

Take time for yourself and discover a more relaxing experience at Lloyds Beach Resort & Spa. The spa offers massage, deep-tissue massage, hot stone massage, sports massage and Swedish massage.

Restaurant & Bar

Eat and drink by the beach

The resort has a restaurant, a bar and a beach bar. Breakfast is served daily from 7:00 AM to 9:30 AM — it is included with some room types, or available for an extra charge. The restaurant serves a dinner buffet, and there is late-night room service.

The menu, food prices and opening hours are confirmed by the resort — enquire and the resort will tell you what is on.
